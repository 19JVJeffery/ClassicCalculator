The Story

A single afternoon.
Forty years of memory.

The Calculator Desk Accessory was one of the first apps shipped with the original Macintosh. Two people made it in a day.

Andy Hertzfeld · 1983

"I wrote it in about an afternoon. It was one of the first Desk Accessories. Susan came over and said it needed to look better."

Andy Hertzfeld — Mac System Software Engineer
Susan Kare · 1983

"I wanted every button to look like it was actually being pressed. The inversion effect gave it that tactile quality you could almost feel."

Susan Kare — Macintosh Graphic Artist & Icon Designer
Timeline
1984

Macintosh System 1.0 ships

The Calculator Desk Accessory debuts alongside MacPaint, MacWrite, and the original Finder. Instantly iconic.

2001

Mac OS X — a new era

Apple ships a full-colour Aqua Calculator. The original black-and-white aesthetic is retired — but not forgotten.

2026

Classic Calculator — reborn

A native SwiftUI recreation. Every pixel traced. Every behaviour matched. Open-source and free.
